# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with Vercel as the intended hosting platform.

## Users

The primary visitors are people verifying the credibility of Baloch Digital and founders evaluating who is behind the fund.

## Product Purpose

The website gives Baloch Digital a credible, professional public presence and a concise way for founders and other counterparties to understand the firm.

## Positioning

Baloch Digital is an early-stage fund focused on frontier technology, specifically crypto, AI, and robotics. It is geographically agnostic with a primary focus on the United States. A more differentiated public positioning is deliberately undecided until the final site copy is supplied.

## Operating Context

Visitors are expected to use the site as a lightweight credibility check, often after encountering Baloch Digital or its founder elsewhere.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The initial product is a simple, single-page fund website.
- Final page structure, copy, and claims will come from a Variant design exported as React or HTML, with screenshots as visual reference.
- Do not invent investment results, portfolio companies, partners, testimonials, credentials, check sizes, or other credibility claims.
- Credibility evidence may be added later, but none is approved for publication yet.
- The existing `balochdigital.io` domain is managed through Squarespace, while email is provided through Google Workspace; future DNS changes must preserve the Google Workspace mail records.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Baloch Digital
- Public domain: `balochdigital.io`
- The implementation should use Impeccable as its design-quality language and review framework.
- User-supplied Variant exports remain the authority for final copy and visual direction.

## Evidence on Hand

No track record, portfolio, testimonials, partners, biographies, or other credibility evidence are approved for publication. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Establish credibility quickly without overstating what is proven.
- Make the fund's frontier-technology focus easy to understand.
- Speak clearly to founders while remaining appropriate for broader credibility checks.
- Preserve factual precision and defer unsupported claims.
- Keep the initial site focused and low-maintenance.
